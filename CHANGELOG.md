### Fixed

- Release smoke supervision cleans up descendants when a command exits before
  its child or responds to TERM while its child ignores it. Linux supervision
  remains active through cleanup instead of assuming the parent's exit proves
  every descendant has stopped.

### Security

- Live interfaces reject non-loopback binds and keep bearer tokens in private
  process and browser-session state.
- Trusted sessions require explicit prepare/confirm, remain memory-only, expire
  after 15 minutes, and never become persistent ACP grants.
- Decision providers cannot grant permissions or activate trusted sessions.
- Automatic routing remains disabled because no provider profile passes the
  accepted activation gate.

No public release has been published. Entries remain under `Unreleased` until a
release is explicitly approved, built, verified, signed, and published.
